# Group contract

Considering the team only consists of two members, organisation is quite straight forward:

The team primarily meets in person and attends the labs together. Aside from the labs, groups meetings happen at least twice a week.

Work is mostly being split for individual work, but includes frequent aligments and more collaborative parts.

Disagreements are going to be discussed, explored, and researched until an agreement can be formed.

Communication is done primarily in person or via Discord.
